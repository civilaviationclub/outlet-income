# Civil Aviation Club · Sales & Stock Costs

Sales and stock-cost statements for Main Bar, Sea View Bar, Restaurant, Shisha and Coffee Shop, including combined BAR totals and a month-by-month report.

Cost of sales = opening stock + purchases + transfers in - transfers out - closing stock. Cost / income = cost of sales / sales x 100. Operating expenses are excluded. Value stock and purchases at cost.

Records stay on each browser/device. No financial records are published. Export private JSON backups regularly. PDF and CSV exports are created locally. Missing months are excluded; total percentages use total cost divided by total sales.

Offline use requires a successful first online load. Clearing browser data removes local records.
